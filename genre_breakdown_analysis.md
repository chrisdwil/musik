## 🔍 Crucial Sub-Level Highlights (The "Anchors")

Within those massive blocks, your curation relies on a few very clear, stable pillars rather than drifting aimlessly:

* 🎤 **The Lyrical Puzzle Core (~20% of Rap block):** A huge density of your alternative rap block is dedicated to independent lyricists like **MF DOOM**, **Aesop Rock**, **Pharoahe Monch**, and **Run The Jewels**. It's complex, intellectual production.
* 👾 **The Chiptune Purist Core (~60% of Electronic block):** Instead of standard club EDM, you heavily bias towards retro hardware aesthetics. **Snail's House**, **Anamanaguchi**, **GameChops**, and **Sabrepulse** hold down an ironclad monopoly here. 
* 🕶️ **The Melancholic Groove Core (~40% of Rock/Synth block):** When your library drops into darker territories, it leans heavily on bass-heavy mood pieces like **The Weeknd's** early works, **She Wants Revenge**, **Boy Harsher**, and **Lana Del Rey**. 
* 🤠 **The Southern Swagger Sub-Block (~15% of Rap block):** You have a highly localized love for Texas/Houston roots, featuring artists like **That Mexican OT**, **Big Pokey**, **Z-Ro**, and classic **UGK**.

## 💡 The Takeaway
You essentially have **three massive, competing musical continents** (Technical Hip-Hop, Pixel-Art Electronic, and Radical Urban Faith) with a dark, atmospheric ocean of **Industrial and Indie Noir** bridging them together. It is incredibly streamlined for a list that is so inherently diverse!
