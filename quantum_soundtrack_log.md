# 🎵 BioShock Infinite: The Quantum Soundtrack Log

This log documents all the anachronistic tracks hidden throughout Columbia, mapping how future hits from the 1960s, 1980s, and 1990s bled back into 1912.

---

### 👾 Part 1: The "Albert Fink" Plagiarized Covers
These tracks were overheard through tears by Jeremiah Fink's composer brother, Albert, and re-arranged into ragtime, barbershop, or calliope styles to fit 1912 society:

*   **"God Only Knows"** — Originally by *The Beach Boys* (1966)
    *   *Location:* Welcome Center / New Eden. Sung by a floating barbershop quartet right at the beginning of the game.
*   **"Girls Just Want to Have Fun"** — Originally by *Cyndi Lauper* (1983)
    *   *Location:* Battleship Bay. Played as a fast-paced, jaunty steam-organ/calliope instrumental on the beach boardwalk.
*   **"Tainted Love"** — Originally by *Gloria Jones / Soft Cell* (1964/1981)
    *   *Location:* Shantytown slums. Playing as a moody, slow-burn acoustic ragtime blues track on a phonograph inside the *Graveyard Shift* bar.
*   **"Fortunate Son"** — Originally by *Creedence Clearwater Revival* (1969)
    *   *Location:* Shantytown. Sung a cappella and hauntingly by a Vox Populi rebel woman sitting on a stoop.

---

### 🌀 Part 2: The Raw Quantum Tears (Original Studio Audio)
These are literal tears ripping through the fabric of space-time. Standing near them allows Booker and Elizabeth to hear the exact original, unaltered future recordings:

*   **"Fortunate Son" (The Rock Original)** — Originally by *Creedence Clearwater Revival* (1969)
    *   *Location:* Plaza of Zeal. Ripping through a tear in a red brick wall right outside the gunsmith shop.
*   **"Girls Just Want to Have Fun" (The Pop Original)** — Originally by *Cyndi Lauper* (1983)
    *   *Location:* Emporia Memorial Gardens. Hidden inside a crumbling, sinking building during a late-game exploration loop.
*   **"Everybody Wants to Rule the World"** — Originally by *Tears for Fears* (1985)
    *   *Location:* Comstock House / The Piano Room. The tear that flashes open right as Elizabeth plays the *C-A-G-E* notes to move the piano obstacle.

---

### 🎬 Part 3: The End Game & Credit Anomalies
Tracks hidden in the final stretches of your journey as Columbia begins to completely fracture:

*   **"Shiny Happy People"** — Originally by *R.E.M.* (1991)
    *   *Location:* Grand Central Depot. Rearranged into a highly ironic, eerie upbeat chamber-orchestra piece looping through the train station speakers.
*   **"Everybody Wants to Rule the World" (The Waltz Arrangement)** — Originally by *Tears for Fears* (1985)
    *   *Location:* The Post-Credits Screen. A beautiful acoustic ragtime-style waltz arrangement that plays to wrap up the entire narrative experience.
